# Interview Questions

Practice concise, structured answers: invariant, assumptions, normal flow, alternatives, failures, telemetry, rollout, and recovery.

## Junior

### 1. Explain and apply 02 DevOps Culture.
**Question**  
How would you use **02 DevOps Culture** in a production backend team or interview answer?

**Expected Answer**  
State the outcome/invariant, implementation boundary, trade-offs, failure cases, evidence, and a proportionate alternative. Connect it to security, reliability, cost, and operational ownership.

**Follow-up Questions**  
- How do retries, concurrency, configuration, or deployment change the design?
- Which test, metric, trace, or runbook proves it works?
- What is the smallest safe rollback or recovery action?

**Difficulty**  
Foundational

**Common Mistakes**  
Listing tools without explaining guarantees; omitting input limits, secrets, failure recovery, or ownership; claiming green CI proves production safety.

**Why Interviewers Ask It**  
It distinguishes memorized terms from practical engineering judgment.
