# Interview Questions

Use each prompt to practice a structured answer: invariant, assumptions, alternatives, failure cases, telemetry, rollout, and rollback.

## Junior

### 1. Explain the request/data path and the invariant this concept protects.
**Question**  
Apply the answer to **03 Non Functional Requirements**.

**Expected Answer**  
State the business and technical invariant, assumptions, normal flow, failure behavior, and measurable success criteria. Compare a simpler and a more resilient alternative, then choose using evidence.

**Follow-up Questions**  
- What changes during a retry storm, deployment, or partial outage?
- Which metric, trace, test, or load profile validates the design?
- What is the smallest safe rollback or recovery action?

**Common Mistakes**  
Reciting product names; omitting timeout/idempotency/data ownership; claiming availability without a failure-domain analysis; ignoring cost and operational ownership.

**Difficulty**  
Foundational

**Why Interviewers Ask It**  
It distinguishes memorized patterns from production engineering judgment.
